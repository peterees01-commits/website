1. Purpose and application

1.1 This Warranty Statement describes the voluntary warranty offered for eligible commercial and infrastructure lighting products supplied by Lumen Method. It applies only where the Quotation, order acknowledgement, product schedule or other written document issued by Lumen Method states that this Warranty Statement applies.

1.2 This Warranty Statement must be read with the Contract, including Lumen Method's Terms & Conditions of Trade, the applicable Quotation, product datasheets, installation instructions and any Manufacturer warranty. If there is an inconsistency, a project-specific written warranty expressly issued by Lumen Method prevails for that project, followed by the Quotation, this Warranty Statement and the Terms & Conditions of Trade.

1.3 Any Manufacturer warranty supplied with the Goods may provide different or additional benefits. Unless Lumen Method expressly states in writing that it gives its own warranty for particular Goods, Lumen Method will pass through or facilitate the applicable Manufacturer warranty and provide reasonable assistance with a valid claim.

2. Warranty coverage

2.1 Subject to this Warranty Statement, Lumen Method warrants that eligible new Goods will be free from material defects in materials and workmanship under normal, intended and specified operating conditions for the applicable Warranty Period.

2.2 A defect is covered only where it materially prevents the Goods from operating substantially in accordance with the applicable published specification. Variations that remain within the product specification, relevant tolerance or accepted lighting-industry practice are not defects.

2.3 Coverage is limited to the components and functions expressly identified in the applicable product documentation. Emergency batteries, sensors, communications devices, control systems, poles, brackets, finishes and other accessories may carry different periods or conditions.

3. Warranty periods

3.2 Unless expressly stated otherwise, the Warranty Period begins on the date the Goods are delivered. Delay in installation, commissioning or energisation does not extend or postpone the Warranty Period.

3.3 A repaired or replacement Good is covered for the unexpired balance of the original Warranty Period, unless a longer period is required by law or Lumen Method agrees otherwise in writing.

4. Conditions of warranty

The voluntary warranty applies only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

  • the Goods were supplied new by Lumen Method and the claimant provides proof of purchase;
  • installation, connection, commissioning and maintenance were performed by appropriately qualified persons in accordance with applicable laws, standards, instructions and good industry practice;
  • the Goods were used only for their intended purpose and within the stated voltage, frequency, ambient temperature, switching, dimming, control, ingress, impact, chemical, vibration, wind, thermal and other operating limits;
  • appropriate electrical protection, earthing, surge protection and control compatibility were provided for the site and application;
  • required maintenance, cleaning, inspection, emergency-lighting testing and record keeping were performed and can be demonstrated;
  • the defect was notified promptly after discovery and before expiry of the Warranty Period; and
  • Lumen Method or the Manufacturer was given a reasonable opportunity to inspect, test and assess the Goods before removal, repair, alteration or disposal.

5. LED performance and tolerances

5.1 Published rated life, L-values, B-values, colour consistency, lumen maintenance and similar performance data describe statistical or laboratory-based performance under specified reference conditions. They are not a promise that every LED, driver or luminaire will operate for the stated number of hours without failure.

5.2 Normal lumen depreciation, colour shift and reasonable differences between production batches are not defects where they remain within the applicable written specification or recognised component tolerance.

5.3 A lighting calculation, maintained illuminance result or compliance outcome depends on assumptions including installation geometry, supply conditions, surface reflectances, environmental contamination, maintenance factors and operating hours. Design or compliance performance is warranted only where Lumen Method expressly issues a separate written project performance warranty.

6. Exclusions

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the voluntary warranty does not cover failure, damage, deterioration or cost arising from:

  • incorrect selection, installation, wiring, polarity, commissioning, programming, configuration, maintenance, cleaning or repair by a person other than Lumen Method;
  • operation outside published limits, abnormal supply voltage, harmonics, transients, surges, lightning, switching events, incompatible dimmers or controls, generators, unstable networks or inadequate circuit protection;
  • water ingress or condensation caused by incorrect assembly, damaged seals, cable entry, site drainage, flooding, pressure cleaning or an application beyond the product's IP rating;
  • corrosion, salt, chemicals, fertiliser, chlorine, solvents, oils, dust, vermin, insects, mould, vibration, impact, vandalism, fire, extreme weather, excessive heat or other adverse site conditions not expressly approved in writing;
  • alteration, disassembly, unauthorised repair, use of non-approved components, removal or defacement of serial numbers, labels or traceability marks;
  • fair wear and tear, cosmetic damage, normal ageing, consumables or maintenance items, including lamps, fuses and batteries except where expressly covered;
  • third-party products, software, communications networks, cloud services, building-management systems, cybersecurity events or interoperability beyond the written specification;
  • failure to follow a written storage, handling, installation, maintenance or commissioning requirement;
  • damage in transit after risk has passed to the Customer, incorrect storage, or prolonged storage in unsuitable conditions; or
  • an event outside Lumen Method's reasonable control.

7. Warranty remedies

7.1 After assessment and subject to any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded, Lumen Method may satisfy an accepted voluntary-warranty claim by repairing the defective Good, supplying a replacement or functionally equivalent product, supplying a replacement component, or providing another remedy agreed in writing.

7.2 A replacement may differ in appearance, dimensions, output, photometric distribution, colour, controls, mounting or other characteristics where the original product or component is no longer reasonably available. Lumen Method will use reasonable endeavours to identify a technically suitable alternative but does not warrant an exact visual or batch match.

7.3 Unless expressly included in the Quotation or required by law, the voluntary warranty covers the defective Good or component only. It does not include diagnosis by third parties, site attendance, isolation, removal, reinstallation, access equipment, traffic management, permits, shutdowns, programming, commissioning, freight, accommodation, labour, making good, loss of use or other consequential project costs.

7.4 No third party may incur costs on Lumen Method's account without Lumen Method's prior written authorisation. Authorised reimbursement is limited to reasonable, evidenced costs and the written approval amount.

8. Making a claim

8.1 Submit the claim to the contact details above, quoting "Warranty Claim" and the project or invoice number.

8.2The claim should include:

  • customer and site contact details, project name and installation address;
  • invoice, purchase order or other proof of supply by Lumen Method;
  • product code, quantity affected, serial or batch numbers and date codes;
  • delivery, installation, commissioning and first-use dates;
  • a clear fault description, failure rate and chronology;
  • photographs and video showing the product, installation, labels and fault;
  • circuit details, supply-voltage readings, surge-protection details, control settings, operating hours and switching profile;
  • installation, commissioning, maintenance and emergency-test records where relevant; and
  • test results or other information reasonably requested for assessment.

8.3 Lumen Method will acknowledge the claim and may request further information, remote testing, return of samples or a site inspection.

8.4 The Customer must not remove, dismantle, repair, discard or return Goods unless safety requires immediate action or Lumen Method first gives written instructions or a return authorisation.

8.5 If Goods are returned and no covered defect is found, or an exclusion applies, Lumen Method may charge reasonable inspection, testing, freight and handling costs after advising the Customer.

9. Safety and urgent failures

If a suspected defect presents an immediate safety risk, the Customer must arrange for an appropriately qualified person to isolate the affected equipment, preserve evidence, prevent further loss and notify Lumen Method promptly. Safety action does not determine whether the claim is covered.

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

10.1 The benefits under this Warranty Statement are in addition to other rights and remedies available under law. Nothing in this Warranty Statement excludes, restricts or modifies a consumer guarantee, right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded, restricted or modified.

10.2 Where the Australian Consumer Law applies, the remedy available and responsibility for associated costs will be determined by that law. Any limitation, exclusion, assessment right or process in this Warranty Statement operates only to the extent permitted by law.

11. Commercial customers and liability

11.1 For acquisitions that are not protected by non-excludable consumer guarantees, Lumen Method's liability under the voluntary warranty is limited to the remedies in section 7 and is subject to the exclusions and liability provisions in the Terms & Conditions of Trade.

11.2 To the maximum extent permitted by law, Lumen Method is not liable under this voluntary warranty for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, revenue, production, contract, opportunity, data, goodwill or anticipated savings.

12. Transfer, changes and governing law

12.1 The voluntary warranty may transfer with the installed Goods to a subsequent owner of the site if proof of purchase, product identity, installation and maintenance records are provided and the Goods have not been relocated, altered or operated outside the original approved application. Personal claims or project-specific commercial benefits are not transferable unless stated otherwise.

12.2 No salesperson, contractor, distributor or other person may extend or vary this Warranty Statement unless the variation is in writing and signed by an authorised representative of Lumen Method.

12.3 This Warranty Statement is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia. The parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Victoria and courts entitled to hear appeals from them.
