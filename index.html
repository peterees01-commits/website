About

About Lumen Method

Our mission is to deliver lighting solutions that balance compliance, performance and practicality—creating environments that are safe, efficient and built to last. Every project is shaped by the needs of the application, the relevant Australian Standards and the long-term performance of the installation.

Successful lighting design is measured by more than calculations and compliance reports. It is measured by how effectively a solution performs throughout its design life, how well it serves the people who use the space and the lasting value it delivers to the asset owner.

We are also committed to sharing our knowledge and supporting the wider industry in the application of lighting principles and Australian Standards. By strengthening technical understanding, we aim to improve the quality and consistency of lighting design across every project.
